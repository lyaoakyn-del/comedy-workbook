# Working with AI

## What AI is Good For (and What It Isn't)

**Good for:**
- Generating punchline alternatives when you're stuck
- Identifying structural weaknesses you can't see yourself
- Finding callback connections across your material
- Analyzing why a joke isn't working
- Drafting set list orders and spotting energy flow issues

**Not good for:**
- Writing jokes from scratch (they'll sound generic)
- Replacing your voice and perspective
- Deciding what's funny (audiences do that)
- First drafts — AI needs your raw material to work with

The rule: **AI develops your observations. It doesn't create them.**

## Setting Up Your AI Buddy

Copy the contents of `CLAUDE.md` into your AI's system prompt:

- **Claude:** Create a Project, paste into Project Instructions
- **ChatGPT:** Settings → Custom Instructions → paste into "How would you like ChatGPT to respond?"
- **Any other LLM:** Paste at the start of every conversation as the system message

Once configured, the AI knows its role and how to work with your material.

## Daily AI Sessions

### Morning Note Analysis (5-10 min)
Paste your morning note. Ask:
> "Read this morning note. Flag the 2-3 observations with the most comedic potential and explain why."

Don't ask it to develop everything — be selective.

### Joke Development (15-20 min)
Paste a joke draft. Ask:
> "Here's a joke I'm working on. Run it through the checklist and give me 3 alternative punchlines."

Or more specifically:
> "The setup feels too long. What can I cut without losing the premise?"
> "The punchline isn't landing. What's the most unexpected direction I could take this?"

### Set List Review (10 min)
Paste your planned set order. Ask:
> "Review this set list for energy flow. Where might I lose the audience? What's the strongest closer from this material?"

## Prompts That Work

**For stuck punchlines:**
> "I have this setup: [setup]. Give me 5 punchlines in completely different directions — absurdist, self-deprecating, observational, dark, and callback."

**For setup surgery:**
> "This setup is too long: [setup]. Cut it to under 2 sentences without losing the premise."

**For finding callbacks:**
> "Here are my 5 strongest bits: [paste bits]. What callback opportunities do you see between them?"

**For material review:**
> "Here are 10 jokes I've been developing. Rank them by stage-readiness and explain your top 3 choices."

---

## Keeping Your Voice

The biggest risk with AI assistance is sounding like everyone else.

After getting suggestions, always ask yourself:
- Would I actually say this?
- Does this sound like me or like a joke-writing algorithm?
- Is this observation mine or has the AI invented a premise I didn't have?

If any answer is "no" / "the algorithm" / "invented" — rewrite or discard.

The AI's suggestions are raw material for your rewriting, not final copy.
